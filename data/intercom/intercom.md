## Overview

Intercom is a customer-support-focused platform with built-in AI agents. Its AI agent Fin can resolve customer queries, take actions across connected tools like Shopify and Salesforce, and continuously improve based on performance data.

## Features

- Fin AI agent for customer support
- Query resolution across connected tools
- Actions across Shopify, Salesforce, and other platforms
- Continuous improvement based on performance
